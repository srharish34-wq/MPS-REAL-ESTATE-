Who We Are

About MPS Realty

MPS Realty is a Chennai-based real estate consultancy offering commercial rentals, property sales, and land investments across key locations such as West Chennai, Central Chennai, South Chennai, OMR, and GST Road with a strong focus on Maduravoyal, Nolambur, Mogappair, Anna Nagar, Kilpauk,Vanagaram, Ambattur, Koyambedu, Arumbakkam, Vadapalani, Kodambakkam ,Ashok Nagar kk Nagar, Ramapuram, Saligramm Valasaravakkam and Porur.

Founded with the vision to make property dealings simple and transparent, MPS Realty provides reliable support for business owners, investors, and property owners alike. We focus on verified listings, genuine client interactions, and smooth documentation to ensure every deal is handled with trust and professionalism.

Our experience across residential, commercial and industrial segments helps clients find spaces that fit their exact needs — whether it's for business expansion, investment, or personal use. We also manage property on behalf of NRI and outstation owners, so an address in Chennai never feels out of reach.
