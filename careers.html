Current Openings

Open Positions

Every role below is based out of our Maduravoyal office, with fieldwork across Chennai.

Property Consultant / Sales Executive

Full-time Chennai Field & Office

Meet clients, show verified properties, and guide buyers and sellers through to a closed deal. Ideal for someone who's comfortable on the phone and on-site.

  • Good communication in Tamil and English
  • Two-wheeler and willingness to travel across Chennai
  • Prior real estate or field sales experience is a plus, not mandatory

Telecaller / Client Relationship Executive

Full-time Chennai Office-based

Handle incoming enquiries, follow up with leads, and coordinate site visits between clients and consultants.

  • Clear, friendly phone manner in Tamil and English
  • Basic computer skills (calls, messages, simple spreadsheets)
  • Freshers welcome

Property Management Coordinator

Full-time Chennai Field & Office

Support NRI and outstation owners — coordinate tenant management, inspections, maintenance requests and rent follow-ups.

  • Organised, detail-oriented, comfortable following up repeatedly
  • Two-wheeler for periodic property visits
  • Basic understanding of rental agreements is a plus

Marketing & Social Media Executive

Full-time Chennai Office-based

Shoot and post property listings, manage our Instagram and WhatsApp presence, and support lead generation campaigns.

  • Comfortable with a phone camera, basic editing and scheduling posts
  • Some experience with Instagram/Facebook page management preferred
  • Creative eye for property photography is a big plus
