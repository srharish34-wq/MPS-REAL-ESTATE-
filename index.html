Ask us directly →
Which parts of Chennai does MPS Realty cover?

We actively work across Maduravoyal, Nolambur, Mogappair, Vanagaram, Anna Nagar, Kilpauk, Koyambedu, Arumbakkam, Ambattur, Porur, Valasaravakkam, Saligramam, Virugambakkam, Kodambakkam, Vadapalani, Ashok Nagar, KK Nagar, Ramapuram, Manapakkam and other prime Chennai locations.

How do you verify a property before listing it?

Every property is personally inspected, and ownership and documentation are checked before it goes on our list, so buyers and tenants deal only with genuine, verified listings.

I live outside Chennai — can you manage my property remotely?

Yes. Our NRI and outstation property management service covers tenant management, rent collection follow-up, maintenance coordination and regular photo and video updates, so you stay informed without being on-site.

What's included in property management?

Tenant finding and verification, rent collection follow-up, rental agreement coordination, maintenance and repair coordination, scheduled inspections, and vacant property monitoring — handled end to end on your behalf.

How long does it take to find a tenant or a buyer?

It depends on the property, location and pricing. Because we market actively and coordinate site visits directly, most owners hear back with genuine interest within the first few weeks — we'll give you a realistic estimate once we see the property.

Is the first consultation chargeable?

No. The first conversation about your property or requirement is free — call or WhatsApp us and we'll take it from there.
