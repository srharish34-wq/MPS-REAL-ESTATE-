Ask us directly →
Do you manage properties for NRI owners?

Yes. Our NRI & outstation property management service is built specifically for owners living outside Chennai or outside India.

Do you manage both residential and commercial properties?

Yes, we manage apartments, independent houses, villas, shops, offices and other commercial properties.

Do you help find tenants?

Yes. Our tenant management service includes finding suitable tenants, profile and document verification, and coordinating the move-in.

Do you coordinate property maintenance?

Yes. We coordinate plumbing, electrical, painting, cleaning, pest control and emergency repairs on your behalf.

Do you inspect vacant properties?

Yes. Vacant properties are monitored and inspected regularly, with photo and video updates shared with the owner.

Do you provide rent collection follow-up?

Yes. We follow up on monthly rent collection and share payment status updates with the property owner.

How frequently will the owner receive property updates?

Update frequency depends on the property and the management plan agreed with you — get in touch and we'll confirm what works for your property.
